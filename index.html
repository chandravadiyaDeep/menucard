Good to know

Questions, answered.

How many menu designs are included?

V1 includes 120 ready-made combinations: 10 layout families with 12 color themes. Your shop details and menu items are automatically inserted into the selected design.

Do I have to type my dishes into every design?

No. Enter your shop profile, categories, dish names and prices once. MenuCard stores that content separately and the template renderer automatically places it into whichever design you select.

Is MenuCard really free?

The core digital menu manager is designed to be free, with no subscription. Optional future hosting or publishing costs will be assessed separately and communicated clearly.

Can I use it without internet?

Yes. Shop owners can open the app and manage their locally saved shop details and menu offline. A customer opening a public menu URL still needs an internet connection.

Will it automatically translate my menu?

No automatic translation is assumed. You can enter Gujarati, Hindi and English versions yourself, and the app uses a clear fallback when a translation is missing.

Can customers scan a QR code right away?

A QR code must point to a real, public HTTPS menu page. QR publishing will only be enabled when that destination exists. The app will not put private data inside a QR code.

Where is my menu data stored?

Core menu data is stored locally on your device. Use the backup/export feature to keep a separate copy. Do not uninstall or clear app storage unless you have a backup.
